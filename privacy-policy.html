Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how InboxUnleash ("the extension", "we") handles your information. InboxUnleash is a Chrome extension that runs inside Gmail and helps small-business owners organize emails, contacts, files, tasks and collections, and manage client conversations with a lightweight one-way CRM — without leaving Gmail. We wrote this document to be open and detailed: what the extension does, where your data goes, and when.

The short version:
  • InboxUnleash runs in your browser against your own Google account. We do not operate a server that receives the content of your emails, files or tasks.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your Google data to train AI models.
  • AI and transcription features are optional. Only when you turn them on, and only after your explicit consent, selected content is sent directly from your browser to an outside provider: Google Gemini (using your own API key), Groq (using your own API key), or Chrome's speech recognition service.
  • Usage data reaches us only if you send it manually, by clicking a button, after previewing what will be sent.
  • You can delete all data the extension created at any time.

3. How we use information

All information accessed through Google APIs is used only to provide user-facing features of the extension, including:

  • Organizing emails into smart folders by keywords, contacts and labels you define.
  • Building lightweight task lists and reminders from email content.
  • Searching message contents and attachment contents (text extraction runs in your browser).
  • Collections management: detecting invoices and payments, tracking money owed to you or by you, and sending payment reminders according to your settings.
  • Displaying contact information, avatars and conversation history.
  • Displaying related calendar events next to email threads, and creating events, reminders and meetings (including Google Meet).
  • Saving web pages and links you choose to your own Google Drive.
  • Translating text and transcribing audio files (see Sections 6–7).
  • Remote control of the system using emails you send to yourself (see Section 4).
  • Sending emails on your behalf only as described in Section 4.
  • Saving your settings and data in your own Google Drive so they persist across devices.

4. Sending emails on your behalf, and remote control

InboxUnleash can send emails from your Gmail account or create drafts in it, in the scenarios below. Emails to other people are sent only through an action you take or a setting you enabled and approved in advance (such as payment reminders, below). No such email is sent unless you turned the feature on.

Emails to yourself (task summaries)

You can ask the extension to email you a summary of your tasks. The email is sent from your Gmail account to your own address.

Meeting invitations

You can create video meetings (via Google Meet) and send invitations to participants. The invitation is sent as a regular email from your Gmail account.

Payment reminders (collections)

If you enable the reminder mechanism for a particular debt, the extension sends payment reminders to the client or other party who owes you money automatically, from your Gmail account and in your name, according to the wording, dates and send time you set and approved in advance. You see the wording before approval, and in the settings you choose, per debt, whether to send automatically or to be asked for approval each time. Reminders may be written with AI assistance (see Section 7) and appear in your Sent folder. When you mark a debt as paid, reminders stop. The extension works only while your browser is open.

Remote control by email

If you enable this feature, you can send yourself an email whose subject starts with @. The extension reads it, performs the requested action (according to the permission level you configured) and replies to you at your own address. These emails are sent to you only.

One-way CRM conversations

InboxUnleash includes a lightweight CRM feature (Customer Relationship Management) that helps you organize your communication with contacts. The chat view is a presentation layer on top of ordinary emails — it is not a separate messaging protocol. Here is how it works:

  • Conversation view: Conversations with each contact are organized in a dedicated view inside the extension, which may appear as a chat-style interface for your convenience. It can be used to start new conversations and to continue existing email threads.
  • Sending messages: When you type a message in this view and click "Send", the extension sends a regular email from your Gmail account to the contact. Messages may include attachments from your existing emails, files you pick from your computer, references to tasks you created, or meeting links — exactly as you would include them in a regular email.
  • The contact's side: The contact receives and replies to an ordinary email. They need no special tool.
  • Optional auto-archive: You can configure the extension to automatically archive specific email threads — removing them from your inbox while keeping them in Gmail's All Mail. This applies only to threads you explicitly chose. Archived threads remain fully accessible in Gmail and can be moved back to the inbox at any time.

All emails sent through the extension appear in your Gmail Sent folder like any other email.

5. Where your data is stored

  • Locally in your browser: The extension stores contacts, folder configurations, tasks, interface state, and email metadata (sender, recipient, subject, date, thread IDs) in the browser's local storage. The extension reads message and attachment contents to run the features described above. The text extracted from files for search is stored in your Google Drive.
  • In your personal Google Drive: Settings, smart folders, tasks, content index, transcripts, saved pages and a usage log (see Section 6) are stored in dedicated folders in your own Drive. This is your data, in your account — we have no access to it.
  • In Gmail itself: All email-related data stays in your Gmail account.

We do not operate a server to which this content is copied. The only exception is sending to the outside providers described in Section 7 — and only when you enabled them.

6. Usage data (statistics) and feedback

  • The extension keeps a feature-usage log only in your Google Drive (the _system folder), so you can see statistics about your own use.
  • We receive usage data only if you choose to send it, manually, by clicking the send button, after being shown a preview of what will be sent. It is sent as an email from your Gmail account to inboxunleash.feedback@gmail.com. There is no automatic sending.
  • A feedback email or bug report you send from the extension reaches us only if you send it.

7. AI features, transcription and third-party services

AI and transcription features are optional. They work only if you entered your own API key (or turned on the browser's built-in transcription), and only after you gave explicit consent in a consent dialog, per content type and per project. You can withdraw any consent at any time.

Google Gemini — AI features

Features such as summaries, suggestions, translation, the personal assistant, invoice analysis and smart actions send the relevant content (for example, excerpts of email bodies, attached files, subjects and contacts you selected) to the Google Gemini API using your own API key. The request goes directly from your browser to Google and does not pass through our servers. Billing and settings are tied to your account and key. Under Google's terms, when you use a free-tier key Google may use the content to improve its products. With a paid key the paid-API terms apply. See the Gemini API terms.

Audio transcription

  • Chrome's built-in speech recognition (default): The audio is played in the browser and sent through Chrome to Google's speech recognition services, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. The extension does not send the audio to our server.
  • Groq Whisper (optional): If you entered your own Groq key and chose this engine, the audio file is sent directly from your browser to api.groq.com. See Groq's Privacy Policy.
  • The final transcript is saved to your Google Drive.

Other services

  • Google APIs — Gmail, Contacts (People API), Calendar and Drive, used as described above and subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — parts of the interface may load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), which receives your IP address like any site serving fonts.
  • Sites you asked to save — when you save a link or page, the extension accesses the site you requested, only after your approval in Chrome.

We do not send your data to any other service. We do not use Google user data to train AI models and do not allow others to do so.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not operate a server that receives or stores the content of your emails, tasks or files.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, other than the providers you turn on yourself (Section 7).
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing targeting.
  • We do not collect analytics about your email content or behavior. Usage data reaches us only if you sent it manually (Section 6).
  • No person on our side reads your messages, files or contacts.

9. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

InboxUnleash's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular: we use the data only to provide user-facing features; we do not transfer it to third parties except to provide the feature you enabled and with your consent; we do not use it for advertising; and we do not allow humans to read it, except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, or as required by law. We do not use Google data to train general AI models.

10. GDPR — users in Europe

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have specific rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Legal basis

Our legal basis for processing your information is your explicit consent, given when you installed the extension and granted the requested Google permissions, and separately when you approved sending content to AI features or transcription. You can withdraw consent at any time (in the extension settings, or by removing the extension and revoking its access — see Section 12).

Data controller

For GDPR purposes, the data controller is InboxUnleash Tech Solutions. To exercise any of the rights below, contact us at inboxunleash.privacy@gmail.com.

Your rights under GDPR

  • Right of access — you can ask what data we hold about you. (We do not hold personal content on our servers; your data is in your Google account and your browser. We hold only usage data or feedback you chose to send.)
  • Right to rectification — corrections are made directly in your Google account.
  • Right to erasure — you can delete all data created by the extension with the "Delete all my data" function in the extension settings. This removes the files the extension created in your Drive and clears the browser's local storage. You can also request deletion of usage data or feedback you sent us by writing to inboxunleash.privacy@gmail.com.
  • Right to data portability — your data is in standard formats in your Google account and can be exported.
  • Right to object — you can object to processing by removing the extension or turning features off at any time.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority.

International data transfers

The extension itself does not transfer data to our servers. If you enable AI features or transcription, content is sent from your browser to Google (Gemini or speech recognition) or to Groq, which may process it outside your country, including in the United States, under their own policies. Enabling these is your choice.

Data retention

  • In your Google account, for as long as you choose to keep it.
  • In the browser's local storage, until you remove the extension or clear browser data.
  • Usage data or feedback you sent us manually is kept in our mailbox and deleted on your request.
  • At Google and Groq — under their retention policies, if you enabled those services.

11. Security

InboxUnleash processes your data in your browser and in your Google account, so security also depends on the security of your Google account and device. API keys you enter (Gemini, Groq) are kept in your browser's storage and are not sent to us. We recommend:

  • Enable two-step verification on your Google account.
  • Keep your browser and operating system up to date.
  • Periodically review connected apps in your Google account settings.
  • Restrict your API keys (quotas, usage limits) with the provider.

12. Your rights and choices

  • Revoke access: You can revoke the extension's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Manage AI consents: You can withdraw any consent to send content to an AI provider in the extension settings, or simply remove your API key.
  • Delete all your data: Use "Delete all my data" in the extension settings to remove all files the extension created in your Drive, together with all locally stored data.
  • Restore archived threads: You can move them back to your inbox at any time using standard Gmail tools.
  • Remove the extension: Removing the extension from Chrome clears the data it stored locally in your browser. Files in your Drive remain until you delete them with the function described above.

13. Children
